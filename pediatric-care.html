Comprehensive Pediatric & Advanced Child Healthcare

At Sri Hasanamba Multispeciality Hospital, we recognize that children require specialized, gentle, and child-centered medical care. Our Department of Pediatric Care & Neonatology provides complete healthcare for children from birth through adolescence, combining medical clinical precision with warmth and empathy.

Led by Dr. Harsha S. B., a renowned consultant pediatrician with advanced fellowship training in neonatology and pediatric intensive care, our department is fully equipped with modern medical technology, including dedicated Neonatal Intensive Care Units (NICU) and Pediatric Intensive Care Units (PICU), ensuring the highest standards of safety for delicate young lives.

Key Capabilities of Our Pediatric Department

  • 24×7 Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU): Dedicated care for premature infants, low-birth-weight babies, neonatal respiratory distress, and neonatal jaundice with advanced phototherapy and servo-controlled warmers.
  • Pediatric Intensive Care Unit (PICU): High-dependency critical care unit with dedicated pediatric ventilators, multipara monitors, and continuous intensivist oversight.
  • Universal Vaccination & Immunization Clinic: Complete vaccination schedule aligned with Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP) and WHO guidelines for newborns, toddlers, and school-age children.
  • Childhood Asthma & Respiratory Care: Specialized diagnosis and nebulization therapy for acute wheezing, bronchitis, childhood asthma, and respiratory allergies.
  • Growth & Neurodevelopmental Monitoring: Routine milestone tracking, pediatric nutritional counseling, management of childhood obesity, and early intervention for developmental delays.
  • Pediatric Emergency & Trauma Resuscitation: 24/7 casualty intake with specialized pediatric emergency drugs, resuscitation bays, and rapid pediatric ambulance response.

Conditions We Diagnose and Treat

Our pediatric team manages both common childhood ailments and complex critical illnesses, including:

Infectious Diseases & Fevers

Viral fevers, dengue, typhoid, urinary tract infections, chickenpox, and acute gastrointestinal infections.

Allergic & Respiratory Disorders

Childhood asthma, allergic rhinitis, croup, pneumonia, and chronic cough management.

Newborn & Infant Conditions

Neonatal jaundice, feeding difficulties, infantile colic, umbilical infections, and prematurity follow-up.

Nutrition & Growth Issues

Faltering growth, iron deficiency anaemia, vitamin D deficiency, rickets, and pediatric dietary planning.

Hospital Facilities

  1. HOME
  2. Facilities

24×7 Emergency & Trauma Care

We treat accidents, acute pain, breathing difficulties, high fever, stroke, cardiac events, and other emergencies with dedicated 24/7 rapid trauma care.

Ambulance Services

There is a 24-hour ambulance service for hospital transfers, emergency trauma pickups, and rapid critical patient transport across Hassan district.

Dialysis Unit

When the kidneys are unable to adequately filter waste materials, our advanced hemodialysis unit provides safe, continuous renal support.

Digital X-Ray

High-definition digital radiography ensuring low radiation exposure, rapid reporting, and crystal-clear diagnostic scans for bones, chest, and organs.

ECG & Cardiac Monitoring

Continuous real-time bedside multi-parameter monitoring, 12-lead ECG, cardiac rhythm evaluation, and pulse oximetry for critically ill patients.

Endoscopy Services

Advanced diagnostic and therapeutic gastrointestinal video Endoscopy, Colonoscopy, and ERCP suite equipped with automated sterilizers.

Modular Operation Theatres

Ultra-sterile modular surgical suites equipped with laminar airflow, HEPA filtration, C-Arm fluoroscopy, and advanced laparoscopic towers.

Intensive Care Units (ICU / NICU)

Multi-disciplinary ICU and Neonatal ICU with high-end mechanical ventilators, invasive arterial lines, and 24/7 critical care physician supervision.

24/7 Diagnostic Pathology Lab

Fully automated Biochemistry, Clinical Hematology, Microbiology, and Hormonal testing instruments ensuring fast, verified, and accurate results.

24/7 In-House Hospital Pharmacy

Fully stocked with genuine prescription drugs, surgical supplies, life-saving critical care medications, and implants at ethical prices day and night.

Comfortable Inpatient Wards & Rooms

Patient-centric accommodation options including AC Deluxe Private Suites, Semi-Private Rooms, and spacious General Wards equipped with oxygen points.

Daycare & Minor Procedure Lounge

Designed for swift same-day admissions and discharges for minor laparoscopic procedures, endoscopy, cataract, and wound dressings with comfort.

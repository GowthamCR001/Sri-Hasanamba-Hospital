Expert Neurological Care & Rapid Neuro-Trauma Stabilization

The Department of Neurology & Neuro Care at Sri Hasanamba Multispeciality Hospital is dedicated to the precise diagnosis, cutting-edge medical management, and rehabilitation of complex disorders affecting the brain, spinal cord, peripheral nerves, and neuromuscular junctions.

Integrated seamlessly with our 24×7 Accident & Advanced Trauma Care Centre and high-dependency Intensive Care Units (MICU/SICU), our neurology service provides life-saving intervention for acute stroke, polytrauma head injuries, intracranial hemorrhages, and status epilepticus, as well as compassionate outpatient care for chronic neurological conditions.

Clinical Capabilities & Specialized Care

  • Acute Ischemic Stroke & Brain Attack Protocol: Rapid-response emergency triage for acute ischemic stroke, emergency neuro-imaging evaluation, and close intensive monitoring in our critical care unit.
  • Neuro-Trauma & Polytrauma Head Injury Care: Comprehensive surgical and medical management of traumatic brain injuries (TBI), concussions, skull fractures, and epidural/subdural hematomas.
  • Epilepsy & Seizure Disorder Clinic: Advanced digital Electroencephalography (EEG), classification of complex focal/generalized seizures, and tailored antiepileptic drug protocols.
  • Chronic Headache & Migraine Management: Specialized diagnostic workup for recurrent migraines, tension headaches, cluster headaches, and non-pharmacological trigger mitigation.
  • Movement Disorders & Parkinsonism: Long-term clinical management for Parkinson's disease, essential tremors, dystonias, and gait abnormalities.
  • Peripheral Neuropathy & Spine Nerve Compression: Evaluation and non-surgical/surgical management of diabetic peripheral neuropathy, sciatica, cervical radiculopathy, and Guillain-Barré Syndrome (GBS).

Conditions We Evaluate & Manage

We provide compassionate, evidence-based care for a wide spectrum of neurological illnesses:

Stroke & TIA

Acute ischemic stroke, transient ischemic attacks (mini-strokes), and intracranial hemorrhagic events.

Epilepsy & Convulsions

Adult and childhood seizure disorders, refractory epilepsy, and febrile convulsions.

Headache & Facial Pain

Refractory migraines, chronic daily headaches, trigeminal neuralgia, and tension headaches.

Nerve & Muscle Disorders

Diabetic neuropathies, myasthenia gravis, muscle dystrophies, numbness, and tingling sensations.

Post-Stroke & Neuro-Rehabilitation

Healing from neurological trauma or stroke requires structured, multidisciplinary rehabilitation. Our specialized physiotherapists and occupational therapy team work directly alongside patients to retrain balance, regain limb strength, prevent joint contractures, and restore daily functional independence.

Proactive Health Protection & Early Disease Detection

At Sri Hasanamba Multispeciality Hospital, we strongly uphold the principle that prevention is always superior to cure. Many major medical conditions — including hypertension, type-2 diabetes, coronary artery disease, fatty liver disease, and gastrointestinal malignancies — develop silently over years before manifesting evident symptoms.

Our Preventive Healthcare Department provides meticulously structured health screening packages tailored for varying age groups, lifestyle risk profiles, and clinical family histories. Utilizing state-of-the-art biochemistry analysers, digital imaging, ultrasonography, and specialized video endoscopy, we deliver rapid, accurate evaluations backed by personalized specialist consultations.

Curated Health Checkup Packages

1. Basic Wellness Screening Package

Annual Routine Check

Ideal for young adults and corporate employees seeking an annual baseline assessment of vitality, organ health, and metabolic balance.

  • Complete Blood Count (CBC) with ESR & Peripheral Smear
  • Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS) & Postprandial Blood Sugar (PPBS)
  • Lipid Profile (Total Cholesterol, Triglycerides, HDL, LDL, VLDL)
  • Serum Creatinine & Blood Urea (Kidney Screening)
  • Complete Urine Routine & Microscopy
  • 12-Lead Resting Electrocardiogram (ECG) & Physician Consultation

2. Comprehensive Master Health Checkup

Most Recommended

Thorough multi-organ investigation designed for adults aged 35 and above, providing detailed evaluations of cardiovascular, liver, and renal parameters.

  • All tests included in the Basic Wellness Panel
  • Complete Liver Function Test (SGOT, SGPT, Bilirubin, Albumin, Alkaline Phosphatase)
  • Comprehensive Renal Function Test (Urea, Creatinine, Uric Acid, Electrolytes)
  • Glycated Hemoglobin (HbA1c - 3 Month Blood Glucose Average)
  • Thyroid Profile (TSH Level)
  • Ultrasound (USG) Examination of Whole Abdomen & Pelvis
  • Digital Chest X-Ray (PA View) & Senior Consultant Physician Consultation

3. Specialized Gastrointestinal & Liver Screening

Center of Excellence

Tailored specifically by our Surgical Gastroenterology Unit for individuals with recurring acidity, indigestion, fatty liver, or family history of GI disorders.

  • Extended Liver Function Panel & Viral Hepatitis Screening (HBsAg, Anti-HCV)
  • High-Resolution Ultrasound of Liver, Gallbladder, Pancreas & Spleen
  • Diagnostic Video Upper GI Endoscopy (when clinically indicated)
  • Stool Routine & Occult Blood Screening
  • Consultation with Consultant Surgical Gastroenterologist

4. Women's Wellness & Senior Citizen Care

Dedicated Care

Age-specific health packages featuring Pap smears, pelvic ultrasound, bone mineral density, and cardiac risk assessments.

  • Comprehensive metabolic & lipid panel with Vitamin D & Vitamin B12 levels
  • Cervical cancer screening (Pap Smear) & Pelvic Ultrasound
  • Calcium & Bone Health screening for osteoporosis
  • Consultation with Consultant Gynecologist & Senior Physician

Lifelong Primary Healthcare for the Entire Family

The Department of Family Medicine & General Healthcare at Sri Hasanamba Multispeciality Hospital serves as the first point of contact for patients seeking reliable, continuous, and compassionate clinical care. We treat individuals of all age groups, providing ongoing care for acute illnesses, chronic medical conditions, and long-term health maintenance.

Rather than viewing symptoms in isolation, our family physicians evaluate the patient holistically, taking into account medical history, lifestyle factors, hereditary risks, and emotional well-being. When advanced surgical or super-speciality care is required, our doctors facilitate seamless internal referrals to our on-campus gastroenterologists, orthopaedic surgeons, cardiologists, and gynaecologists.

Core Services of Family Medicine

  • Chronic Disease Management & Diabetology: Comprehensive glycemic monitoring, HbA1c tracking, insulin titration, and prevention of diabetic complications (neuropathy, retinopathy, nephropathy).
  • Hypertension & Cardiovascular Risk Profiling: Blood pressure control, lipid profile management, cardiac risk stratification, and lifestyle modification guidance.
  • Acute Illness & Seasonal Fever Clinic: Rapid diagnosis and evidence-based treatment for dengue, malaria, typhoid, viral upper respiratory infections, bronchitis, and urinary tract infections.
  • Thyroid & Endocrine Disorders: Screening, medical management, and hormonal balancing for hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, and metabolic syndromes.
  • Geriatric & Elderly Healthcare: Specialized care for senior citizens managing multiple chronic conditions, age-related mobility challenges, osteoporosis, and comprehensive prescription medication reviews.
  • Adult Immunization & Travel Vaccinations: Tetanus, Influenza (Flu), Pneumococcal vaccines for seniors, Hepatitis B, and Typhoid immunizations.

Specialized Family Care Programs

We offer targeted clinical programs to help families maintain optimal health throughout every chapter of life:

Comprehensive Diabetes Clinic

Regular blood glucose monitoring, dietary planning, foot care assessments, and prevention of kidney and nerve complications.

Hypertension & Heart Health

Continuous blood pressure monitoring, ambulatory tracking, ECG evaluation, and cardiovascular risk reduction.

Senior Citizen Wellness

Attentive, unhurried consultations for elderly patients, managing arthritis pain, sleep quality, and medication optimization.

Lifestyle & Weight Counseling

Clinical nutrition plans, medically supervised weight reduction, stress management, and tobacco cessation programs.

24/7 Diagnostic & Inpatient Integration

Our Family Medicine OPD is supported by state-of-the-art diagnostic facilities under one roof. Patients can access complete blood and biochemical testing, digital X-rays, ultrasound imaging, ECG, and echocardiography with same-day report turnarounds. Prescribed medications are available immediately from our 24/7 in-house pharmacy, eliminating the need to visit multiple facilities.
